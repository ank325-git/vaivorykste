Apartment in Palanga with a pool, terrace and a quiet Kunigiškės setting

Vaivorykštės Poolside Apartment is a cosy apartment in Kunigiškės, Palanga, designed for a quiet seaside stay. It sleeps up to 4 guests and includes a separate bedroom, a living area with a sofa bed, a fully equipped kitchen, shower, towels, Wi‑Fi and private parking.

The key advantage is the private terrace with direct access to a heated outdoor pool. It is a practical option for guests looking for an apartment in Palanga with a pool: calmer than the central resort area, but still close to the beach, cycling paths and Kunigiškės leisure areas.

The apartment is well suited for couples, families with children or a small group of guests looking for short-term accommodation in Palanga. For available dates, pricing and reservation details, contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email — direct booking can often provide a better price than booking platforms.

Frequently asked questions

How many guests can stay in the apartment?

The apartment comfortably sleeps up to 4 guests: a double bed in the bedroom and a fold-out sofa bed in the living room.

Is the pool heated?

Yes, the outdoor pool is heated, so swimming is comfortable throughout the warm season. The private terrace has direct access to the pool.

How far is the sea?

The Kunigiškės beach is about a 10-minute walk away along a quiet path through the pine forest.

Is parking available?

Yes, guests have a free private parking space with gated access.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 15:00, check-out until 11:00. Different times can be arranged individually.

Are pets allowed?

Unfortunately, pets are not allowed – we want to keep the apartment comfortable for all guests, including those with allergies.
